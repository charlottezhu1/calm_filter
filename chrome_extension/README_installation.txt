Calm Filter: installation
=========================

1. Download and unzip the folder.
2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" in the upper right corner.
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the chrome_extension folder.
5. Confirm that Calm Filter appears in the list and is enabled.
6. Open x.com. The distribution bar appears near the compose button.

Click the Calm Filter icon to open the switches (Hide HAN posts, Color post borders, Affect distribution). Changes apply immediately.

After updating the extension files, click the reload icon for Calm Filter in chrome://extensions and refresh x.com.
